# ShapeMe legal documents

German and English legal/support pages for ShapeMe, provided by
**TORCHTECHNOLOGY LTD**, trading as **Torch Technology**.

Published with GitHub Pages at **https://pyfinn.github.io/shapeme-legal/**:

- [Privacy Policy](https://pyfinn.github.io/shapeme-legal/privacy/)
- [Terms of Use](https://pyfinn.github.io/shapeme-legal/terms/)
- [Legal Notice](https://pyfinn.github.io/shapeme-legal/impressum/)
- [Support](https://pyfinn.github.io/shapeme-legal/support/)
- [Delete account and data](https://pyfinn.github.io/shapeme-legal/delete-account/)

The editable source lives in `legal/` in the ShapeMe app repository. The site uses
relative links, `.nojekyll`, and Pages from `main` at `/`, without a custom domain.
Company details were checked against https://torchtechnology.de/de/impressum on
2026-09-20. Contact: hello@torchtechnology.de.

The pages describe optional email/Apple accounts, separately enabled cloud backup,
meal AI processing, subscription management and separate deletion/export paths.
Publication does not certify legal review, processor agreements or production
backend testing; remaining release requirements are tracked in the app repository.
