12. Data Protection

Information on the processing of personal data in ShapeMe can be found in the separate Privacy Policy at https://shapeme.torchtechnology.de/privacy. The Privacy Policy is not part of this Legal Notice. In particular, the Privacy Policy describes:

  • AI-assisted meal analysis: when a meal is analysed, the meal photo or text description is transmitted – only for the duration of the analysis – via the provider's backend (Supabase) to an AI service (including in the USA, e.g. OpenRouter, Inc. as a sub-processor routing to model providers such as Google) in order to estimate nutritional values. The provider does not store meal photos or meal content on its servers. The estimates are approximate, user-editable and do not constitute medical advice;
  • abuse limiting for the AI feature via short-lived, hashed (SHA-256) IP addresses with hourly and daily counters – no user identity, no photo and no meal content is stored;
  • the absence of any user account: ShapeMe requires no registration, and profile, body-metric, training, weekly-plan, achievement and nutrition data is stored exclusively locally on the device and is not transmitted to the provider's servers;
  • subscription management exclusively via Apple (payment / card data is collected by Apple and never reaches the provider); offer codes are redeemed through Apple's own redemption flow;
  • optional daily reminders delivered as local notifications, scheduled and processed entirely on the device;
  • international data transfers to the USA and the applicable safeguards, the legal bases, retention periods and the rights of data subjects (including the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority).
