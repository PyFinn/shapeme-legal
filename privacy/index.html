Nach gesonderter Freigabe über Cloud-Backup aktivieren speichert die App eine Momentaufnahme deines Profils einschließlich Körperdaten, Trainings, Mahlzeiten, Plänen, Vorlagen und Fortschritt bei Supabase. Hinzu kommen Konto-ID, Gerätename, App- und Formatversion sowie Zeitpunkt der Sicherung. Foto-Miniaturbilder werden vor dem Upload entfernt. Eine neue Sicherung ersetzt die vorige; es handelt sich nicht um ein Archiv sämtlicher Versionen.

Die Freigabe wird pro Konto auf diesem Gerät mit Zeitpunkt gespeichert. Bestehende Installationen erhalten keine automatische Freigabe. Rechtsgrundlagen sind die angeforderte Sicherung (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. b DSGVO) und deine ausdrückliche Einwilligung für Gesundheitsdaten (Art. 9 Abs. 2 lit. a DSGVO).

Cloud-Backup pausieren oder Abmelden stoppt weitere Uploads. Bereits gespeicherte Sicherungen bleiben bestehen. Für einen vollständigen Widerruf einschließlich Entfernung der gespeicherten Gesundheitsdaten verwende Konto löschen oder kontaktiere uns. Die übrige App bleibt ohne Backup nutzbar.

Eine generelle Zusage „keine Speicherung bei allen Anbietern“ wird nicht gemacht. Ergebnisse sind Schätzungen und kein medizinischer Rat.

2. Data on your device

ShapeMe stores your profile (including name, age, sex, weight, height, goals and equipment), plans, exercises, logged sets, meals and nutrients, photo thumbnails, templates, progress and settings locally. It uses these to calculate training and nutrition targets. The app does not read Apple Health or HealthKit data. This history is not uploaded as a backup unless cloud backup is enabled. AI requests and subscription management can separately transmit data as described below.

Providing the requested local functions serves performance of the contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Body and nutrition data may constitute health data. In the current app, we request separate, explicit consent before transmitting them for cloud backup or AI analysis (Article 9(2)(a) GDPR). Accepting terms alone does not replace that consent.

3. Optional account

The free account is separate from paid Pro access. For email sign-in, Supabase processes your email, a password hash and user ID. For Sign in with Apple, we receive the address or relay address and identity information Apple shares; we never receive your Apple password. iOS session credentials are stored in Keychain.

Purposes are sign-in, email verification, password recovery and account security (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not use the address for newsletters or advertising. Under the PKCE sign-in flow, password-reset links must open on the device that requested them.

4. Cloud backup

After separate approval using Enable cloud backup, the app stores a snapshot of your profile including body data, workouts, meals, plans, templates and progress with Supabase, together with account ID, device name, app/format version and backup time. Photo thumbnails are removed before upload. Each new backup replaces the previous snapshot; this is not an archive of all versions.

Your choice and its time are stored for your account on this device. Existing installations are not automatically opted in. Automatic uploads also require reconciliation with any existing backup. Restoring replaces local data; the app offers a choice when meaningful local data already exists. Legal bases are the requested backup service (Article 6(1)(b)) and your explicit consent for health data (Article 9(2)(a) GDPR).

Pause cloud backup or signing out stops further uploads. Existing backups remain. To withdraw fully and remove stored health data, use Delete Account or contact us. Other app functions remain available without backup.

5. Optional AI meal analysis

After confirmation in a separate analysis dialog, the selected photo or description and model instructions pass through a Supabase server function to OpenRouter and the model provider (currently Google/Gemini). Processing can occur in the USA. The request includes no ShapeMe account ID or RevenueCat identifier, although personal information you include in the content may identify you. Photograph only the meal and avoid faces or documents.

Legal bases are consent under Article 6(1)(a) and, for health data, Article 9(2)(a) GDPR. You may decline and enter nutrients manually. Each new request needs confirmation. Previously transmitted requests cannot be recalled retroactively. Our server function does not store photos or descriptions as database records; the AI services’ terms govern their processing and possible retention. We make no blanket promise of zero retention by all providers. Results are estimates, not medical advice.

7. Subscriptions and technical security

Apple processes payments; we do not receive complete payment or card details. RevenueCat processes a randomly generated app identifier and purchase/subscription data to provide Pro (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). The identifier is pseudonymous; ShapeMe does not link it to your Supabase account or email. The app uses no advertising ID and does not track you across other companies’ apps.

To protect the AI service against abuse, IP addresses are processed and hashed IP keys, time windows and request counters are stored (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; legitimate interests in security and cost control). A new request after a time window resets the counter but does not automatically delete its row. These keys are pseudonymous, not guaranteed anonymous. Infrastructure operational logs may also contain connection data.

8. Permissions and website preferences

Camera and photo access are used for your requested meal input. Reminders are local notifications; revoke permissions in iOS settings. The website stores only your selected language in local browser storage and includes no analytics or advertising cookies added by us. Necessary storage provides the requested function; in Germany, Section 25(2) TDDDG applies.

9. Retention, export and deletion

Local data remains until erased in the app or removed from your device; you separately manage operating-system backups and exports. The account and its current cloud backup remain until account deletion. Successful deletion removes both from the active database; infrastructure backup copies and legally required records may follow separate retention cycles. Support correspondence is retained as needed for the request or statutory recordkeeping.

Settings → Data → Export My Data creates JSON including local meal thumbnails. The share sheet sends it to a destination you choose; creating the export does not send it to our servers. The device copy may differ from an older cloud backup; request a copy of server data by contacting us.

Delete Account removes the account and cloud backup, keeps local data and does not cancel an Apple subscription. Erase All Data removes local data and signs you out, but keeps the cloud account. Cancel subscriptions separately through Apple. Our deletion instructions also cover requests without app access. Independent Apple/RevenueCat billing records are not automatically deleted with the ShapeMe account.
